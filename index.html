    Test it on you

    Five steps before you keep a change.

    Faulkner et al. (2024) found the same 1–3 cm change made some riders faster and others slower. Every number here is a starting point.

    1. Change one thing at a time: saddle height, then setback, then front end.
    2. Film yourself side-on at race effort, or use the knee check below.
    3. Ride 2–3 weeks before judging comfort. Trained riders adapt to aero positions.
    4. Same course, same power, compare speed. An aero sensor or velodrome laps give CdA directly.
    5. Keep the change only if you're faster without new pain.
